What is real, what is estimated

Cities rarely weigh waste ward by ward, so most inputs here are estimates. We show them as ranges and let you replace them.

Computed

These follow from the inputs by arithmetic or optimisation.

  • Collection loops and distance saved
  • Facility allocation within capacity
  • Mass balance: every tonne accounted for
  • Fuel, energy and CO₂ from stated factors

Estimated

No sensors, so these come from audits and published ranges.

  • Ward-level waste history
  • Wet, dry, e-waste split
  • Festival and rain uplift
  • Share sorted at source

Yours to replace

Edit any value in the table below and everything updates.

  • Weighbridge or truck logs
  • A waste audit from your wards
  • Real facility capacities
  • Your fleet's actual mileage
